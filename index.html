Connecting the field to space, and data to decisions

Ground sensors meet NASA Earth science.

AgroNova Orbit is a community-driven smart farming decision-support system. An ESP32-based field node measures soil moisture, air temperature and humidity, NPK nutrients, soil temperature, GPS location, rainfall, light, and crop height (VL53L0X laser sensor). These local measurements are combined with NASA Earth-observation data: NASA POWER, SMAP, GPM IMERG, Landsat/MODIS, ECOSTRESS, and SRTM.
